Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information We Collect

GoRead2 collects only the personal information needed to sign you in, provide the RSS reading service, and process subscription payments.

Google Sign-In

GoRead2 uses Google sign-in and does not store passwords. When you sign in, we receive:

  • Your Google account ID (to identify your account)
  • Your email address (to identify your account and for billing)
  • Your name and profile picture URL (for display)

Data We Store

  • Your account details from Google, listed above, and the date your account was created
  • Your RSS feed subscriptions
  • Which articles you have read and starred
  • Your reader settings
  • Your subscription status: trial end date, last payment date, next billing date, and the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers for your account
  • Sign-in sessions: a random session identifier with its creation and expiry times

This data is stored in Google Cloud, which hosts GoRead2. Google Cloud also keeps request logs that include IP addresses and browser user agents. These logs are used for security and troubleshooting and are deleted automatically after a limited period.

Cookies and Local Storage

GoRead2 sets only the following cookies and browser storage entries, none of which are used for tracking:

  • session_id (strictly necessary): keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only, so page scripts cannot read it. It expires after 7 days without use.
  • oauth_state (strictly necessary): protects the Google sign-in flow against cross-site request forgery. It expires after 10 minutes and is deleted once sign-in completes.
  • tabletHelpDismissed (local storage, functional): remembers that you dismissed the tablet help message. It is never sent to our servers.

Analytics

GoRead2 does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Payment Processing

Payments for GoRead2 Pro are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe:

  • Data shared with Stripe: your email address and name, to create your Stripe customer account
  • Card details: entered on Stripe's checkout page and handled entirely by Stripe. They are never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Retention: Stripe keeps payment and customer records according to its own retention policies and legal obligations

For details, see Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Other Third-Party Requests

Some content loads directly from other servers, which receive your IP address and browser details:

  • Google Fonts: the Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • jsDelivr: DOMPurify, the library that removes unsafe markup from articles, is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network. jsDelivr does not set cookies.
  • Profile pictures: your Google profile picture is loaded from Google's servers
  • Article images and media: images in articles load directly from each feed publisher's servers. Feeds themselves are fetched by GoRead2's servers, so publishers do not learn which feeds you subscribe to.

How We Use and Share Data

We use your information only to provide GoRead2 and process subscription payments. Apart from the services named above (Google Cloud hosting, Google sign-in, Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and Stripe), we do not share, sell, or distribute your personal information.

Data Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account exists, including your full read and starred history
  • Sessions: expire after 7 days without use. Expired sessions are deleted automatically.
  • Payment records: some payment records are kept by Stripe after account deletion, as required for tax and fraud prevention

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: download a copy of the personal data we hold about you, as a JSON file, from the Your Data section of the account page
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and its data
  • Portability: export your feed subscriptions as an OPML file, which other RSS readers can import, from the account page or the GoRead2 iOS app
  • Subscription management: cancel your subscription at any time from the account page or Stripe's customer portal

To make a correction or deletion request, contact us using the details below.

Contact
